1. Warm greeting

Hello, everyone! Welcome to Türkiye! I’m a student here, and today I’m talking to a visitor.

Hi! Thank you. I’m a tourist, and this is my first day in Türkiye!

2. Introducing ourselves

My name is . I’m Turkish, and I speak Turkish and English. The capital city of my country is Ankara. And you?

Nice to meet you! I’m . I’m British, and I speak English. The capital city of my country is London.

3. Interview questions

, which city should I visit first?

Istanbul! It’s on two continents. You can visit Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque and the Grand Bazaar.

What can I do in Cappadocia?

You can take a hot air balloon ride at sunrise and explore the fairy chimneys.

Now it’s my turn. , what do you want to do in Türkiye?

I want to swim at the beaches in Antalya and walk through the ancient city of Ephesus.

What traditional food do you want to try?

I don’t know much about Turkish food. What do you recommend?

Start with a Turkish breakfast: cheese, olives, tomatoes, eggs and simit. For lunch, try a kebab or lahmacun, and have baklava for dessert.

Wow, I’m hungry already! Is Turkish tea special?

Yes! We drink çay in small tulip-shaped glasses. Offering tea to guests is a sign of hospitality.

4. Confident goodbye

Thank you for watching. Enjoy your stay in Türkiye!

Teşekkürler! Hoşça kalın, bye!

Places in our dialogue

Tourist attractions we talk about.

Istanbul

History and city life

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Grand Bazaar and a ferry across the Bosphorus.

Cappadocia

Landscape

Fairy chimneys, underground cities and hot air balloons at sunrise.

Antalya

Sea and old town

Mediterranean beaches and the historic Kaleiçi district.

Ephesus

Ancient city

Roman streets, a huge theatre and the Library of Celsus.

Pamukkale

Nature

White terraces filled with warm thermal water.

Food and drink

What our tourist tries in Türkiye.

Turkish breakfast

Morning

Cheese, olives, tomatoes, eggs and fresh bread on a big table.

Kebab

Main dish

Grilled meat, often served with rice, bread and salad.

Lahmacun

Street food

A thin, crispy flatbread with minced meat and herbs.

Simit

Snack

A round bread covered with sesame seeds, sold on the street.

Baklava

Dessert

Thin layers of pastry with nuts and sweet syrup.

Çay

Drink

Turkish tea in small tulip-shaped glasses. Offering it is a sign of hospitality.
